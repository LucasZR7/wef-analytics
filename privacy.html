Privacy policy — WEF Analytics

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's consent, WEF Analytics reads aggregate statistics of the When Engineering Fails YouTube channel (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) using the read-only scopes yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

Whose data. Only the channel owner's own channel. It does not collect personal data about viewers or any other user; YouTube Analytics returns aggregate numbers only.

Storage and sharing. The numbers are stored on the channel owner's own computer for internal analysis. They are not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

WEF Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
